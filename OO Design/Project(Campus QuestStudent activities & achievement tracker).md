## Who is our main user?
- Primary Users: Freshmen
    - New to university life, overwhelmed by campus layout, seeking social connections and navigation clarity. They will use it to engage themselves to students life.
- Secondary Users: Upperclassmen.
    - They are not really interested in this feature but still some people will want to track the progress, build a portfolio of achievements, stay engaged with university events, and earn some bonuses.
- Campus Administration
    - They will track the progress of students.
## What problem are we solving?
We are solving the issue of discomfort during the first days of university life, as freshmen often feel lost and isolated during their first weeks. Through the platform, they can learn more about the campus, make friends along the way, and participate in student activities. We also solve the problem of fragmented information (which is currently scattered across multiple websites and flyers) by consolidating it into a single page.
## What is the main workflow?
Sign Up: A student logs in with their university account and picks their major.
Find a Quest: They open the app to see a single feed or map of events, locations, and challenges.
Complete the Action: The student goes to the location and completes the task by scanning a QR code or doing the activity.
Get Rewarded: The app instantly gives them points and badges, which they can trade for perks like university merch or cafeteria discounts.
Track Attendance: In the background, university administration can see which events and spots students are visiting most to make more quests.
## What is our MVP?
Student Accounts: Basic login using student emails and password.
One Consolidated Feed: A simple list of campus locations and orientation events so students don't have to look through flyers or scattered sites.
Basic Quest Check-ins: Scanning QR codes at event booths or checking in via GPS to complete tasks.
Points & Badge System: A basic counter showing total points earned and digital badges collected.
Simple Rewards: A static list of rewards students can claim with their points.
Basic Admin Panel: A simple web page for staff to add new events and download a spreadsheet of student participation.
Login->Activities->Quest check-in->Get points for completing the task->Can buy something in the shop for the points.
